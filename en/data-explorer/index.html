Who is truly gaining ground?

A bank can show positive growth while losing market share. ABIX distinguishes absolute growth, relative position and contribution to sector dynamics.

  • Market share

    Calculated on major financial aggregates

  • Gain or loss

    Share evolution in percentage points

  • Contribution

    Bank's share in sector growth

  • Growth gap

    Relative performance compared to market average

Measure banking market concentration

Track the evolving weight of leaders and the transformation of the competitive market structure.

  • CR1, CR3, CR5, CR10

    Cumulative weight of the sector's largest players.

  • HHI (Herfindahl-Hirschman)

    International benchmark concentration index.

  • Effective number of banks

    Indicator of the actual diversity of market players.

  • Cumulative key players

    Number of banks needed to reach 50%, 75% or 90% of the market.

Discover the methodology

Versioned data

Organized by bank, indicator, year and edition.

Rigorous missing data handling

Absent data remains N/A and is never automatically treated as zero.

Centralized calculations

Ratios, CAGRs, market shares, percentiles and benchmarks rely on homogeneous rules.

Traceability

Results can be linked to their analytical source and parameters.

A comparative analysis tool, not a prudential rating : ABIX Data Explorer provides comparative financial analysis based on available data. It does not replace a full credit analysis, regulatory prudential ratios, or information on asset quality and regulatory liquidity when not publicly available. This reinforces the product's methodological credibility.

These dimensions and analyses are provided for comparative and methodological purposes. They must never be interpreted as a credit rating, regulatory rating, official solvency measure or probability of default.
