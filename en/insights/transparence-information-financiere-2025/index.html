ABIX Insights

Why this analysis?

The availability of financial information represents an important dimension in observing a banking institution.

Beyond analyzing financial performance, ABIX also examines how institutions make their financial statements and activity reports accessible to the general public.

This analysis provides an overview of their availability and level of timeliness on the official websites of the surveyed banks.

Analysis carried out within the framework of ABIX – Algeria Banking Index, with the contribution of Mr. Sekak Rachid.

Methodology

A standardized assessment measuring the public accessibility of two benchmark documents on official websites.

01

Publication of financial statements on website

Maximum score: 3 points

Evaluation of the availability and timeliness of financial statements published on the institution's official website.

02

Publication of activity report on website

Maximum score: 3 points

Evaluation of the availability and timeliness of the annual activity report accessible on the institution's official website.

Overall score

Financial Statements + Activity Report = score out of 6

The score aims to measure the digital availability of the observed information, and not the intrinsic quality of the published content.

How to interpret these results?

This analysis focuses exclusively on information identified on the official websites of the institutions during the review conducted by ABIX. The absence of an identified document on a website does not necessarily mean that it has not been produced, submitted to competent authorities, or distributed through other channels. The score measures the online availability and timeliness of accessible information. It constitutes neither an assessment of the intrinsic quality of accounts, nor an evaluation of regulatory compliance, governance, or financial strength.

State of play at the time of analysis

The results reflect the information identified on the official websites of the institutions at the time of analysis. As banking websites are subject to evolution, results may be updated when an institution provides new documents or brings to our attention information that was not identified during the review.

What the results show

The results reveal heterogeneous practices regarding the availability of financial information on banking websites.

Several banks offer recent, easily accessible financial statements and activity reports, whereas for other institutions, the identified information is older, partial, or was not found during the review.

This overview highlights the value of integrating published information availability into the new observation dimensions of the Algerian banking sector.

Vision ABIX 2026

ABIX expands its scope of analysis

ABIX – Algeria Banking Index is no longer limited solely to the analysis of financial performance.

The ecosystem is evolving towards a broader perspective on the Algerian banking sector, progressively integrating new dimensions of analysis, notably the availability of published information and customer experience.

This new overview constitutes one of the dimensions that will enrich the 2026 edition.
