The First Publication at the Origin of the Initiative

Published in 2023, this report represents Tadjeddine & Partners' first publication dedicated to comparative observation of the Algerian banking sector.

Using 2021 and 2022 financial data, it provides an overview of key indicators of banks operating in Algeria, institutional comparisons, and key market observations.

This initial initiative progressively evolved toward more structured in-depth analyses, leading to subsequent studies and the creation of Algeria Banking Index.
