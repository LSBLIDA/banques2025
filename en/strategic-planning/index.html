Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Corporate Pack include strategic planning?

Yes. The three-year strategic-plan preparation and management module is included in the Corporate Executive Pack.

Does ABIX use my bank’s financial data?

ABIX prefills financial data available in the platform. The bank may supplement and correct it with sourced internal data.

Is artificial intelligence mandatory?

No. The ABIX engine provides standardised generation without AI. AI assistance is optional and subject to consent before any data transmission.

Is the plan approved automatically?

No. Executive Management and the Board of Directors remain responsible for validation and approval.

Must existing Corporate subscribers pay extra?

No. Existing Corporate subscribers receive the new module at no additional charge during their current subscription.

Can progress against objectives be tracked?

Yes. The module compares three-year targets with annual actual results.
