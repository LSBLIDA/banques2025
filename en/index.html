Studies, benchmarks and strategic analyses of the Algerian banking sector

Algeria Banking Index is the editorial brand created by Tadjeddine & Partners to publish independent studies, benchmarks and strategic analyses dedicated to the Algerian banking sector.

The 2026 edition analyses the 2024–2025 performance of 21 banks operating in Algeria, with a perspective on 2023–2025 trends. It combines financial data, operational indicators, benchmarks, competitive dynamics and new insights on banking usage.
