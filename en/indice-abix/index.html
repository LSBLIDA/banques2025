Project Under Design & Validation

ABIX — A Banking Index Under Development

Tadjeddine & Partners is developing ABIX, a future comparative index designed to synthesize multiple performance dimensions of banks operating in Algeria.

This project is separate from sector studies currently published under the Algeria Banking Index brand.

Important — Project in Development

Methodology, indicators, weightings, and calculation formulas are currently undergoing testing and validation. No final ABIX scores or rankings are published at this stage.

Envisioned Objectives for the Index

Provide a multidimensional reading of banking performance
Facilitate synthetic peer comparisons across institutions
Track performance evolution over time
Complement detailed annual study reports
Offer a synthetic sector benchmarking framework

The 5 Dimensions Currently Under Study

Financial Performance

Return on Equity (ROE), Return on Assets (ROA), Net Banking Income, and Gross Operating Profit.

Operational Efficiency

Cost-to-Income Ratio, branch network productivity, and general expense management.

Commercial Growth

Lending dynamics, deposit collection, and market share evolution.

Financial Soundness

Equity levels, risk coverage, and liquidity ratio structures.

Asset Structure

Balance sheet composition, loan portfolio distribution, and asset quality.
