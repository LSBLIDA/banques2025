Privacy Policy

Personal data protection and confidentiality commitments of EURL Tadjeddine & Partners.

1. Data Controller Identity

In accordance with Law No. 18-07 of June 10, 2018 on personal data protection, the data controller is TADJEDDINE AND PARTNERS, RC 10B0981001, Cité Naimi, Rue G N° 1, Zabana, Blida, represented by Managing Director Mr. Tadjeddine BACHIR.

As data controller, TADJEDDINE AND PARTNERS complies with all applicable personal data protection laws.

2. Collected Data

TADJEDDINE AND PARTNERS collects and processes the following user data:

  • First and last name
  • Professional email address
  • Phone number
  • Organization / bank / company name
  • Job title / Position
  • Website and stated requirements

3. Purposes of Processing

Collected data is processed exclusively to:

  • Respond to user inquiries, order requests, and information demands
  • Send commercial proposals tailored to stated needs (ABIX study, advisory, benchmarks)
  • Manage pre-contractual and contractual relationships
  • Perform internal business statistics and analyses

4. Data Recipients

Personal data is processed exclusively by authorized staff of TADJEDDINE AND PARTNERS and is never sold or rented to third parties.

Any technical sub-processor engagements (e.g. hosting, email dispatch) are governed by strict confidentiality agreements conforming to Law No. 18-07.

5. Data Retention Period

Personal data is retained for up to three (3) years following the last interaction, unless consent is withdrawn or erased per legal rights.

6. Security Measures

TADJEDDINE AND PARTNERS implements appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to ensure confidentiality, integrity, and availability of processed personal data.

7. Rights of Data Subjects

Under Articles 7 and 34–36 of Law No. 18-07, individuals have the following rights:

  • Right of access to personal data
  • Right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to object on legitimate grounds
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at:

Email : info@tadjeddine-partners.com

Par voie postale : TADJEDDINE AND PARTNERS, Cité Naimi, Rue G N° 1, Zabana, Blida – Algérie.

8. International Data Transfers

No personal data is transferred abroad without prior authorization under Law No. 18-07.

9. Policy Updates

TADJEDDINE AND PARTNERS reserves the right to update this policy to maintain regulatory compliance.
