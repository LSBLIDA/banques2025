About ABIX

Algeria Banking Index is an initiative by Tadjeddine & Partners to provide Algerian decision-makers with an independent analytical reference for the banking sector.

What is ABIX?

ABIX — Algeria Banking Index — is an index and an annual comparative study of banks operating in Algeria. It analyzes financial performance based on published disclosures to provide leaders, analysts, and investors with an objective, structured benchmark.

ABIX does not constitute a regulatory or prudential rating. It is a strategic decision-support tool grounded in transparency and methodological rigor.
